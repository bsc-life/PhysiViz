`multicellds.py` and `config.py` in this directory are a minimal vendored
subset of [pctk](https://github.com/PhysiBoSS/pctk) (the PhysiCell ToolKit),
by Miguel Ponce de Leon, under the PhysiCell project's BSD 3-Clause license
(see `LICENSE` in this directory). They are unmodified apart from being
copied out of the full pctk package. Only `MultiCellDS` (the MultiCellDS
reader used by `export_crossfeeding_viz.py`) is used from pctk here.
